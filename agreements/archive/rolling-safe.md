# Rolling SAFE, reference example

A Rolling SAFE stays open continuously instead of closing a round: an investor buys at the
price shown on a dashboard at the moment of purchase, set by a published linear function,
and holds tokens that convert on a liquidity, dissolution or company-elected conversion
event. Key terms as executed: Rolling SAFE Equity Percentage 50%; price = Effective
Purchase Price from the dashboard curve; Fiat transfers reprice if the curve moves more
than 5% before funds land.

See `agreements/README.md` for how this fits the component clauses.

2.  Purchase of Tokens and Voluntary Company Conversion.
3.  As consideration for the Investor paying the Purchase Amount and executing this Rolling SAFE on the Site, the Company shall issue, and record via book entry or electronic form, to Investor a number of COOK Rolling SAFE Tokens (the “Tokens”) equal to the Purchase Amount divided by the Effective Purchase Price. The Company shall similarly issue to future Rolling SAFE Investors a number of Tokens determined by dividing the purchase amounts under their respective Rolling SAFEs by the Effective Purchase Price. In addition, the Company shall issue Tokens to certain other holders of convertible equity instruments (the “Prior Convertibles”) and previously issued digital assets of the Company, in such amounts as mutually agreed by and between the Company and such investors. Tokens shall have no rights other than the rights provided in this Rolling SAFE and any subsequently executed transaction agreements between the Investor and the Company.
4.  If the Board of Directors of the Company elects to voluntarily convert this Rolling SAFE prior to the termination of this Rolling SAFE, on the day following the date of such election via written consent or pursuant to a Board of Directors meeting validly held by the Company, this Rolling SAFE will automatically convert (such conversion, the “Voluntary Company Conversion”) into a number of shares of Rolling SAFE Preferred Stock equal to the Pro Rata Share of the Allocated Rolling SAFE Preferred Stock. In connection with the Voluntary Company Conversion, the Investor will execute and deliver to the Company all of the transaction documents related to the Voluntary Company Conversion as requested by the Company. All payments due under this Rolling SAFE may be made in the forms and using the methods indicated on the Site which shall include but is not limited to, (A) payment by virtual currency from Investor’s digital wallet maintained on the Site and (B) by bank wire, ACH or SEPA transfers in immediately available funds to a bank account designated by Company (a “Fiat Transfer”). All payments hereunder shall be denominated in U.S. dollars.
6.  In the event that Investor tenders the Purchase Amount using a Fiat Transfer, Investor hereby acknowledges and agrees that this Rolling SAFE will not be deemed complete and executed until the Company has confirmed receipt of such Fiat Transfer in the account previously provided to Investor. Accordingly, the Effective Purchase Price indicated to Investor on the Site as of the date the Investor initiates the Fiat Transfer (such price, the “Original Effective Purchase Price”) may materially differ from the Effective Purchase Price as of the date the Company confirms receipt of the Fiat Transfer (such date the “Fiat Transfer Confirmation Date”). Investor hereby acknowledges this risk and consents to the Company automatically processing Investor’s purchase of the Tokens at the Effective Purchase Price in effect as of the Fiat Transfer Confirmation Date so long as the Effective Purchase Price in effect as of such Fiat Transfer Confirmation Date is within 5% of the Original Effective Purchase Price.
7.  If the difference between the Effective Purchase Price in effect as of the Fiat Transfer Confirmation Date and the Original Effective Purchase Price exceeds 5%, the Company shall contact Investor pursuant to the notice provisions provided herein (or at the most recent contact information Investor has provided on the Site) and request that Investor reaffirm their interest in purchasing the Tokens at the Effective Purchase Price in Effect as of the Fiat Transfer Confirmation Date pursuant to a method of confirmation determined by the Company in its sole discretion. In the event that the Investor confirms their intent to proceed with the purchase of Tokens at such new Effective Purchase Price, the Company shall process the Investor’s payment and Investor shall receive Tokens equal to the Purchase Amount divided by the Effective Purchase Price in effect as of the Fiat Transfer Confirmation Date. If the Investor indicates that they do not wish to purchase the Tokens at the Effective Purchase Price in effect as of the Fiat Transfer Confirmation Date, the Investor shall be eligible to withdraw their funds in accordance with the Company’s instructions provided to Investor, less applicable fees incurred by the Company in processing Investor’s Fiat Transfer.
8.  In connection with any Fiat Transfer, the Company shall be allotted up to six (6) business days to confirm receipt of the Investor’s Fiat Transfer and process such payment, provided that if the Effective Purchase Price as of the date of the Fiat Transfer Confirmation Date is within 5% of the Original Effective Purchase Price, the Company shall be permitted to process such payment in accordance with Section 1(a)(iii)(B) above, regardless of how many business days have elapsed since Investor has initiated the Fiat Transfer. If (i) the Fiat Transfer Confirmation Date is more than six (6) business days from the date that the Investor initiated the Fiat Transfer and (ii) the difference between the Effective Purchase Price in effect as of the Fiat Transfer Confirmation Date and the Original Effective Purchase Price exceeds 5%, the Company will follow the notice provisions set forth in Section 1(a)(iii)(B) above, and Investor shall be entitled to withdraw their funds from the Site and cancel this Rolling SAFE.
9.  Liquidity Event.
10. If there is a Liquidity Event before the termination of this Rolling SAFE, this Rolling SAFE will automatically be entitled (subject to the liquidation priority set forth in Section 1(d) below) to receive a portion of Proceeds (if any) that are due and payable to the Rolling SAFE Investors as the result of such Liquidity Event. Subject to the liquidation priority set forth in Section 1(d) below, the amount payable in the event of a Liquidity Event shall be equal to the greater of (A) the Purchase Amount and (B) the amount payable on the number of shares of Common Stock equal to the Pro Rata Share of the product of multiplying (x) the Rolling SAFE Equity Percentage and (y) the Liquidity Capitalization (the “Conversion Amount”).
11. Notwithstanding the foregoing, in connection with a Change of Control intended to qualify as a tax-free reorganization, the Company may reduce the cash portion of Proceeds payable to the Investor by the amount determined by its board of directors in good faith for such Change of Control to qualify as a tax-free reorganization for U.S. federal income tax purposes, provided that such reduction (A) does not reduce the total Proceeds payable to such Investor and (B) is applied in the same manner and on a pro rata basis to all securityholders who have equal priority to the Investor under Section 1(d).
12. Dissolution Event. If there is a Dissolution Event before the termination of this Rolling SAFE, the Investor will automatically be entitled (subject to the liquidation priority set forth in Section 1(d) below) to receive a portion of Proceeds equal to the greater of (i) the Purchase Amount and (ii) the Conversion Amount, due and payable to the Investor immediately prior to the consummation of the Dissolution Event.
13. Liquidation Priority. In a Liquidity Event or Dissolution Event, this Rolling SAFE is intended to operate like standard non-participating, Preferred Stock. The Investor’s right to receive the greater of (x) the Purchase Amount and (ii) its Conversion Amount is:
14. Junior to payment of outstanding indebtedness and creditor claims, including contractual claims for payment and convertible promissory notes (to the extent such convertible promissory notes are not actually or notionally converted into Capital Stock);
15. On par with payments for other SAFEs and/or Preferred Stock who are also receiving Conversion Amounts or Proceeds on a similar as-converted to Common Stock basis, and if the applicable Proceeds are insufficient to permit full payments to the Investor and such other SAFEs and/or Preferred Stock, the applicable Proceeds will be distributed pro rata to the Investor and such other SAFEs and/or Preferred Stock in proportion to the full payments that would otherwise be due; and
16. Senior to payments for Common Stock.
17. Rolling SAFE Equity Percentage Adjustment. In the event that the Company issues Tokens to one or more Rolling SAFE Investors in connection with the conversion of Prior Convertibles (such Tokens to be issued, the “Conversion Tokens”), the Rolling SAFE Equity Percentage shall be increased, concurrently with such issuance, to a new percentage (calculated to the nearest ten thousandth of a percent) in accordance with the following formula:

CP2 = CP1 \* (A + B)/A.

For purposes of the foregoing formula, the following definitions shall apply:

1.  “CP2” shall mean the Rolling SAFE Equity Percentage in effect immediately after such issuance or deemed issuance of Conversion Tokens;
2.  “CP1” shall mean the Rolling SAFE Equity Percentage in effect immediately prior to such issuance or deemed issuance of Conversion Tokens;
3.  “A” shall mean the number of Tokens outstanding immediately prior to such issuance or deemed issuance of Conversion Tokens (treating for this purpose as outstanding all Tokens reserved but unissued by the Company); and
4.  “B” shall mean the number of Conversion Tokens to be issued to a Rolling SAFE Investor in connection with the conversion of a Prior Convertible, including the Investor’s Prior SAFE.
5.  Termination. This Rolling SAFE will automatically terminate (without relieving the Company of any obligations arising from a prior breach of or non-compliance with this Rolling SAFE) immediately following the earliest to occur of: (i) the issuance of Capital Stock to the Investor pursuant to the automatic conversion of this Rolling SAFE under Section1(a)(iii); or (ii) the payment, or setting aside for payment, of amounts due the Investor pursuant to Section 1(b) or Section 1(c). In the event of a termination of this Rolling SAFE, all rights and obligations of this Rolling SAFE which by their nature should survive termination will survive termination, including such applicable rights and obligations of the parties under Sections 3-5 and the attached exhibits referenced therein. Right of First Refusal: As soon as practicable, the Company will deliver to each holder of Rolling SAFE Preferred (i) an unaudited balance sheet as of fiscal year end and (ii) unaudited statements of income and cash flows for such fiscal year. Optional Conversion: Rolling SAFE Preferred converts 1:1 to a series of Common Stock at any time at option of the holder, subject to adjustments for stock dividends, splits, combinations and similar events and, if applicable, as described under the “Anti-dilution” section of this Term Sheet.
