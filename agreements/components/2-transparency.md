# 2 — Transparency, not permission

## Clauses, as written

**Practice** — [budgets-and-payments-2022](../archive/budgets-and-payments-2022.md)
> We are committed to transparency - you can always know how much money we have, and to see
> what we spend it by examining the labels in taiga.

**Bylaws 3.3** — [corporate-bylaws](../archive/corporate-bylaws.md)
> Team Members shall have the right to inspect books, accounts and records of the
> Corporation at any time with reasonable advance notice, with the exception of personnel
> records of individuals.

**Bylaws 3.7**
> The list shall be open to the examination of any TeamCookHolder for any purpose germane to
> the meeting, during ordinary business hours, for a period of at least ten (10) days before
> the meeting, at an online address that is accessible by the URI given to TeamCookHolders.

**[Transparency Not Permission](../reading.md)**
> "With enough eyes, all bugs are shallow" applies to more than code. Visibility into
> resource allocation and decision making can be as powerful as voting, without the delay.
> Trust can be placed in project leads who move fast in the open, until and unless an
> objection is raised.

**[Playing on an Organic Team](https://linkedtrust.us/blog/playing-on-an-organic-team/)**
> budget is justified by placing amounts on peer-reviewed tasks that are transparent to all
