## The FI model, as their page states it

<https://fi.co/equity>

> companies pledge 2.5% of their future equity in the form of a Warrant

Split, per that page: 0.5% to mentors "for providing feedback and guidance both during and
after the Agentic Program", 1% to FI HQ, 1% to local leaders. Signed "approximately
two-thirds of the way through the program", not at the start. 60% of returns go back to
local leaders and mentors.
