# Reading

- **[Grow like Trees](https://linkedtrust.us/blog/grow-like-trees-an-organic-approach-to-societal-regrowth/)** — "No law insists that corporations must be top-down." Voting power tied to contribution; sortition — rotating committees randomly selected from equity holders.
- **[Playing on an Organic Team](https://linkedtrust.us/blog/playing-on-an-organic-team/)** — "budget is justified by placing amounts on peer-reviewed tasks that are transparent to all"; "The executive assigning budgets must be accountable to the full team via a periodic vote of confidence."
- **[Just because we agree to something, it doesn't mean it's fair. Things change.](https://linkedtrust.us/blog/just-because-we-agree-to-something-it-doesnt-mean-its-fair-things-change/)** — the creator of Slicing Pie on splits fixed at founding.
- **[The Very Meaning of Teamwork](https://linkedtrust.us/blog/the-very-meaning-of-teamwork/)** — "everyone is willing to pitch in a hand when needed".
- **Transparency Not Permission: DAO Tools for Flow** — "Trust can be placed in project leads who move fast in the open, until and unless an objection is raised." <https://docs.google.com/document/d/1R2sode63KhD3WEiyhI10nwfB0TyaQ1Cn9G9Wow6lAZE/edit>
- **Slicing Pie** (Mike Moyer) — GovKit exports to it. The gift edition is not in this repo.
- **[docs/governance-practices.md](../docs/governance-practices.md)** — the three mechanisms in this repo.
