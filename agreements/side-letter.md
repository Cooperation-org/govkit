# Side letter to the SAFE, Workers.VC form

[Date]

[Investor Name]
[Address]

Re: SAFE dated [Date of SAFE] (the "SAFE") between [Company Name] (the "Company") and the Investor

In connection with the Investor's purchase of the SAFE, the Company and the Investor agree as follows.
Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the SAFE.

**1. Founders pool.** Before the SAFE converts or is paid out, the Company will reserve under its equity
incentive plan a number of shares equal to [55]% of the Company Capitalization at the Post-Money Valuation
Cap (the "Founders Pool"), for grants to persons who provide reviewed work to the Company. The Founders Pool
is part of the Unissued Option Pool, Options and Promised Options, so grants from it do not reduce the
number of shares the Investor receives under the SAFE.

**2. Voting.** The Safe Preferred Stock and any shares the Investor receives on conversion of the SAFE carry
no vote, except as required by law. The Company's certificate of incorporation and bylaws give votes to
persons who earn them by work. All economic rights of the Safe Preferred Stock are as stated in the SAFE.

**3. Amendments.** In addition to Section 5(a) of the SAFE, the SAFE and this letter may be amended by
written consent of the Company and the holders of a majority, by Purchase Amount, of the Safes with the same
Post-Money Valuation Cap that respond within 30 days after the Company sends written notice of the proposed
amendment to every such holder. The notice must offer a way to respond electronically, including a way to
object. Responses must come from holders of at least [one third] of that Purchase Amount. The Purchase Amount
and the Post-Money Valuation Cap may not be amended this way, and every amendment must treat all such holders
in the same manner.

**4. No other changes.** Except as stated in this letter, the SAFE is unchanged. If this letter and the
SAFE conflict, this letter controls.

## Why a side letter

Investors and their counsel know the YC post-money SAFE line by line. Signing it unmodified, with the three
Workers.VC differences in one short letter, keeps every other term standard and keeps the differences in one
place. The earlier full form (`safe.md`) is kept for reference.
