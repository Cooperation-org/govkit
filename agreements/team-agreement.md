# Earned governance: agreement

Team ______________ · value unit ______ · weight window ______ · date __________

Each clause is adopted or struck. The words are from the documents in
[archive/](archive/); the numbers are the ones a team sets.

---

**1 · Work becomes ownership** · adopt ☐ strike ☐ · [component](components/1-work-becomes-ownership.md)
> The Task Tracker must have a method for peer review and approval of tasks.
> Issued no more often than **14** days, no less often than **90**. Published **7** days
> before issuance. No more than **10%** of the existing total per **30** days.

**2 · Transparency, not permission** · adopt ☐ strike ☐ · [component](components/2-transparency.md)
> you can always know how much money we have, and to see what we spend it by examining the
> labels in taiga
> Members may inspect books, accounts and records at any time with reasonable advance
> notice, other than personnel records of individuals.

**3 · Opportunity to Object** · adopt ☐ strike ☐ · [component](components/3-opportunity-to-object.md)
> communicated with **7** days notice with voting to conclude no more than **14** days after
> the original notice · passed if those who vote approve, even if a quorum do not
> participate · default **51%** · any group holding **5%** or more may call a vote over any
> action · denied actions may be proposed again after **30** days.

**4 · Work-weighted, ranked-choice voting** · adopt ☐ strike ☐ · [component](components/4-work-weighted-voting.md)
> Under no circumstance may [the voting token] be purchased or received by means other than
> performing work that was reviewed and approved on the Task Tracker.
> Ranked where more than two options.

**5 · Steering by lot** · adopt ☐ strike ☐ · [component](components/5-steering-by-lot.md)
> rotating, work-weighted sortition, not elected · advisory · **3** seats, **1** rotating
> per period.

**6 · Investors** · adopt ☐ strike ☐ · [component](components/6-investors.md)
> Money can buy SAFE-like slices over future equity, but not governance.

**7 · Mutual grants** · proposed, not adopted · [component](components/7-mutual-grants.md)
> granting equity to Workers.VC itself, if the accelerator program was useful - say $1k
> equity - do folks agree? And then Workers.VC granting equity to the cohort members if
> they gave valuable feedback. FI has a thing where all members grant some to each other.
> do we want that. but can do by task and by reaction in slack/discord in future so its
> granular not automatic

**Changing this** · Opportunity to Object, **60%** of the vote.
